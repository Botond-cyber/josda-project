If you're new

Some answers arrive as questions.

A little clarity about the experience, your readings and your ritual.

What is Dawn Oracle?−

Dawn Oracle is a quiet space for reflection through Tarot, Moon Oracle, I Ching and Numerology. Ancient symbols become thoughtful, personalized interpretation, helping you see your question from another angle.

Is this fortune telling?+

We don't tell you your future. We help you see it differently. Readings are exploratory reflections, not fixed predictions or promises about what will happen.

How do the readings work?+

Bring a question, choose an Oracle and a method, then draw your cards or explore the symbols. The interpretation connects those symbols to your question.

Can I ask any question?+

You can explore love, life, relationships, career, choices and personal growth. Open questions invite richer reflection.

How accurate are the readings?+

A reading offers a perspective, not a measurable forecast. Its value lies in the patterns you notice and the questions it opens.

Can I cancel anytime?+

Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time. Your plan remains available until the end of your current billing period.

Do you save my readings?+

First Light includes limited reading history. Moonlight includes reading history and a personal journal.

What is the difference between the methods?+

Tarot explores archetypes and choices, Moon Oracle listens to emotion and cycles, I Ching offers perspective on change and balance, and Numerology explores symbolic patterns.
